# Live Research, Candidate Status, And Expiry

Read this reference whenever `audio-story-youth-trend-language` is activated. Do not select a time-sensitive term before completing it.

## Research Window

- Fast meme or viral catchphrase: prefer evidence from the last 7-45 days.
- Platform slang or current phrasing: prefer the last 30-120 days.
- Durable youth vocabulary: confirm meaningful use within the last 6-18 months.
- Revived/recycled trend: verify active return, not a new article explaining old usage.

Publication date alone is not evidence of current use.

## Source Hierarchy

Prefer current original/organic use, platform trend pages, public posts and comments, search/social-listening data, original creators, and current community pages. Recent Vietnamese journalism, youth publications, industry analysis, and careful explainers may support meaning and origin.

Do not rely alone on generic SEO lists, copied glossaries, shopping pages, undated pages, AI-generated glossaries, or articles whose only evidence is old posts.

For each time-sensitive term, confirm at least two independent signals among:

- recent real use;
- a current explanation;
- current search/social attention;
- use by more than one independent community;
- platform-specific context;
- current comments showing shared understanding.

Use stronger verification for terms that may insult, sexualize, stereotype, accuse, politicize, or create legal/reputational harm.

## Search Protocol

Search the exact term together with its meaning/origin, the current year, a relevant Vietnamese platform/community, and `Việt Nam`. Also look for counter-evidence using concepts such as `hết trend`, `lỗi thời`, `cringe`, `dùng sai`, or `tranh cãi`.

Never report the search process inside the story.

## Candidate Card

Record internally:

```text
Term and normalized spelling:
Intended pronunciation / VoxCPM form:
Literal and pragmatic meaning:
Origin and current platform/community:
Status and evidence date:
Audience age/region:
Emotional tone and common position:
Safe/unsafe context and harm risk:
Evergreen substitute and expiry risk:
Character owner, scene, and narrative function:
```

## Status

- `RISING`: gaining visible use; meaning may still shift.
- `CURRENT`: broadly active now.
- `NICHE-CURRENT`: active in a defined community.
- `ESTABLISHED`: no longer new but naturally understood.
- `REVIVED`: old phrase actively returning.
- `LEGACY`: recognizable but dated.
- `SATURATED`: so overused it risks sounding commercial or forced.
- `DECLINING`: understood but losing freshness.
- `UNCERTAIN`: evidence insufficient or contradictory.

Prefer `CURRENT`, `NICHE-CURRENT`, or `ESTABLISHED`. Use the others only for a deliberate character, period, community, or staleness effect.

## Shelf Life

- Immediate release: current fast memes may be used after verification; record date and recheck before release.
- Six-to-twelve-month life: prefer established current terms and avoid creator-specific catchphrases.
- Evergreen catalogue: use durable colloquial language; fast trends only as intentional period markers.

Recheck when more than 30 days have passed since research.

Keep a private ledger:

| Term | Status | Evidence date | Character | Scene | Function | VoxCPM form | Uses | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|

The ledger prevents one term spreading to every character, stale wording surviving revision, and inconsistent spoken forms.
