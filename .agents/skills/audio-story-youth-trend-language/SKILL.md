# Youth and Trend Language

This skill controls vocabulary only. It never repairs plot, motivation, chemistry, emotion, pacing, genre payoff, or exposition.

## Activation and Research

Use only for an explicit fiction request mentioning slang, teencode, Gen Z/Gen Alpha, memes, trends, viral phrasing, `nói lái`, or youth speech.

Before selecting a time-sensitive term, research current usage, meaning, tone, platform/community, offensiveness, pronunciation, and whether it is already stale. User examples are candidates, not automatic approval.

If browsing is unavailable, do not claim a term is current. Prefer durable colloquial Vietnamese.

Read `references/research-and-expiry.md` when the skill activates.

## Ownership

Every term must belong to a speaker and social action:

- Would this person know it?
- Are they sincere, ironic, late, wrong, or performing youthfulness?
- Who are they speaking to?
- Are they flirting, mocking, softening, hiding, bonding, or gaining status?
- Would they use it with a parent, boss, lover, and stranger?

A neutral narrator should not sound like a comment section. Historical/secondary-world characters do not automatically understand modern slang unless the premise establishes it.

## Density

Use the smallest amount that creates age, relationship, setting, humor, or status. Density notes are ceilings, never targets:
- serious drama/mystery/horror: usually rare;
- youth romance/office/campus comedy: selective clusters;
- high comedy/social-media scenes: denser only when all speakers share the context.

Return to plain speech between noticeable terms. A scene with none may be best.

## Meaning and Context

Use current pragmatic meaning, not dictionary similarity. Social labels are POV judgments until evidence confirms them.

A listener should infer the term's emotional/social role from the surrounding line without a dictionary pause.

Transform trend wording through this character and scene; do not paste a viral caption. Avoid trend language in death, abuse, medical crisis, legal evidence, trauma disclosure, or sincere confession unless defensive humor is clearly character-driven and the gravity remains.

## Audio/TTS

Decide the written spoken form for English-origin words, stylized spelling, acronyms, and `nói lái`. Keep it consistent and test recurring unfamiliar terms in a representative VoxCPM sample.

Do not insert hashtags, emoji, raw platform UI, or production markup into spoken prose unless the story literally depicts them.

## Gate

Keep a term only when speaker fit, situation fit, current meaning, listener inference, density, shelf life, and TTS form all work. Remove it when it makes the line less human, less clear, or more dated than the intended story.
