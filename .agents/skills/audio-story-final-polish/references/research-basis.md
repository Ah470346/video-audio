# Research Basis And Synthesized Principles

This reference stores the reasoning basis for adjusting the skill. It is not a checklist to read on every final-polish run.

## 1. Coherence Comes From Causality

Narrative-comprehension research suggests recipients integrate events into a causal structure; comprehension rises when new events link strongly to earlier events. Classic work on "causal cohesion" also found events inside a causal chain are remembered better.

- Song et al., *Cognitive and Neural State Dynamics of Narrative Comprehension*, Journal of Neuroscience (2021): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34531284/
- Trabasso et al., *Causal Cohesion and Story Coherence* (1982): https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED214147

**Editorial inference:** the "therefore/but" test and trigger -> choice -> consequence chain are more useful than surface transition words.

## 2. Characters Live Through Goals And Choices

Purdue OWL emphasizes that characters need clear goals; without goals, they lack motivation to act, and conflict should obstruct those goals. UCD's revision guidance puts goal and failure cost early in character-development repair.

- Purdue OWL, *Writing Compelling Characters*: https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction-basics/writing_compelling_characters.html
- UCD Writing Centre, *Revising and Editing Fiction*: https://www.ucd.ie/writingcentre/t4media/Creative%20Writing%20Revising%20and%20Editing%20Fiction.pdf

**Editorial inference:** every scene should have want, obstacle, choice, and change; clues and victories should not fall into a character's hands without action.

## 3. Natural Dialogue Needs Context And Subtext

UCD recommends using action and stage position to reveal emotion, avoiding over-explanation through adverbs, and reading dialogue aloud. The Center for Fiction emphasizes dialogue doing several jobs at once, carrying conflicting goals, tactic shifts, and meaning beneath the words.

- UCD Writing Centre, above, section *Revising Dialogue*.
- The Center for Fiction, *Tips for Writing Dialogue*: https://centerforfiction.org/writing-tools/tips-for-writing-dialogue/

**Editorial inference:** dialogue should be checked through goal, tactic, subtext, distinct voice, and read-aloud tests. "Sounds real" does not mean keeping every filler word.

## 4. Vietnamese Address Forms Are Relationship Data

Ton's dissertation analyzes conversation data and shows Vietnamese address choice and shift express attitude/emotion in context; choice depends on relationship, age, social status, culture, and emotional state. Research in *Ngôn ngữ và Đời sống* also describes kinship terms as central and flexible in Vietnamese social communication.

- Ton, *Vietnamese terms of address: Pragmatic connotations, translation and ESL/EFL pedagogy* (2017): https://rune.une.edu.au/entities/publication/8d369b85-034e-4c75-a948-006a986f66cc
- Nguyễn Văn Khang, *Addressing in Vietnamese with kinship term and their use in public services communication*: https://vjol.info.vn/index.php/NNDS/article/view/20274

**Editorial inference:** address forms cannot be fixed by a one-word replacement table. Build pair matrices, track relationship temperature shifts, and evaluate shades of `nó/hắn/tôi/cô/anh/em` in context.

## 5. Emotion Needs Immersion, Not Only Labels

Research on emotional transportation links story immersion with empathy effects. This does not prove any single wording formula, but it supports keeping listeners inside an experience rather than pulling them out with author explanation.

- Bal and Veltkamp, *How Does Fiction Reading Influence Empathy?* PLOS ONE (2013): https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0055341

**Editorial inference:** prioritize rooted reaction, threatened goals, and context that lets listeners infer feeling; do not mechanically replace emotion with body-symptom lists.

## 6. Common Weaknesses Of AI-Generated Stories

One study comparing archetype-pattern stories found AI can keep surface structure relatively well while experts rated it lower in emotional depth, character development, originality, and subtlety; familiar patterns tend to repeat.

- Kabashkin, Zervina and Misnevs, *AI Narrative Modeling: How Machines' Intelligence Reproduces Archetypal Storytelling*, Information (2025): https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/16/4/319

This is a narrow study, not a claim about every model or genre. The skill uses it as a signal to create separate passes for psychology, choice, formulaic phrasing, and specificity; it does not treat the label "AI" as proof that a sentence is bad.

## 7. Principles Built Into The Skill

1. Repair macro to micro: logic -> scene -> emotion -> dialogue/address -> sentence -> read-aloud.
2. Evaluate events by causality and character decisions, not only time order.
3. Evaluate emotion by root, self-protection strategy, and cost, not number of emotion words.
4. Evaluate dialogue by what characters want and avoid saying, not only grammar.
5. Evaluate Vietnamese address forms by changing pair relationships.
6. Use the linter for surface signals; keep semantic judgment with the agent.
7. Rerun final polish after any content edit because changing one link can break knowledge, causality, or address forms elsewhere.
