# Foreknowledge and Rule Premises

## Identify the Variant

Do not merge distinct mechanics:

- **Reincarnation/regression:** memory of one lived branch, not perfect omniscience.
- **Foreknowledge:** prediction/data/dream/prophecy with defined accuracy and context.
- **Time loop:** experience persists across defined resets.
- **System:** goals/rules/rewards/interface; not automatically truthful or future-knowing.
- **Book transmigration:** knowledge from a specific text/version/POV.
- **Pure transmigration:** old-world knowledge/identity without future plot knowledge.
- **Quick transmigration:** multiple worlds plus a continuing cost/goal/identity thread.

## Premise Contract

Lock:

```text
Source of knowledge or ability:
Scope and accuracy:
What cannot be known or done:
Primary time/change model:
What persists and what diverges:
Risk/cost of use or discovery:
How this advantage forces a different genre choice:
```

If the last line is empty, the premise is decorative.

Choose one primary model: fixed timeline, editable timeline, new branch, loop reset, or text-as-limited-source. Do not switch models to rescue the climax without prior evidence.

## Knowledge Ledger

For decisive scenes track:
- what the character remembers/reads/predicts;
- source and certainty;
- what current actions have already changed;
- which old knowledge may no longer apply;
- action taken and new result.

Mark memory, report, inference, and forecast differently. `Tôi biết` must not hide a guess.

Other characters respond to the new branch as people, not old chess pieces. Foreknowledge never grants the right to remove their agency.

## Systems and Interfaces

Give stable, distinct names to the hidden agent, visible frame, message, input, command, and effect. At first use, show how the character perceives and interacts with it.

Define observation scope, rewards, punishments, truthfulness, loopholes, and limits. Points matter only when they change access or choices. A system should create negotiation, interpretation, resistance, or cost—not make the protagonist a task executor.

## Book/Pure/Quick Transmigration

Book knowledge is limited by version, POV, shown scenes, and memory. Text is not world; off-page people have current goals.

For pure transmigration, knowledge must fit the character's real education and available resources.

For quick transmigration, each world needs a local arc and the series needs a changing throughline: identity, memory, system relationship, goal, or price.

## Suspense and Payoff

Known outcomes shift suspense toward execution, proof, ethics, whom to save, adaptation, divergence, and cost.

The premise must pay through a choice that changes the main genre outcome. Origin explanation is optional unless promised.

## Breakers

- pure transmigration treated as knowing the plot;
- system treated as omniscient without evidence;
- exact memory available only when convenient;
- original plot forces people against motive;
- timeline model changes silently;
- rewards become any power the scene needs;
- old knowledge stays perfectly accurate after large divergence;
- another special-knowledge character added only to restore difficulty;
- mechanism replaces relationships and lived world.
