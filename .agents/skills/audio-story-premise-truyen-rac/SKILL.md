# Deliberate `Truyện Rác`

## Activation

Use only when explicitly requested. Do not relabel logic errors, flat characters, or awkward prose as an intentional style.

Choose the leading experience:
- frustration waiting for release;
- absurdity for laughter;
- retrospective recognition of blindness;
- exaggeration as satire/critique.

Main genre still owns the ending.

## Design Chain

Use:

`root need -> bias -> wrong interpretation -> choice -> consequence -> self-justification or change`

`They are stupid` is not a motive. A character may be competent in one area and blind in a specific attachment, status, duty, or fear.

Lock:
- who behaves excessively;
- what they protect;
- how bias distorts facts;
- who bears the cost;
- which normal laws/reactions still hold;
- what release the listener is promised.

## Reality Anchors

The world must react through money, law, work, trust, witnesses, procedure, status, or physical consequence. Absurd behavior may be heightened; narration and causality remain clear.

Escalate by commitment and cost, not by changing the type of foolishness whenever plot needs movement.

## Frustration and Agency

Track:
- intensity of harm;
- duration before relief/progress;
- agency of the harmed person.

When all are unfavorable for too long, provide a meaningful action, ally, clue, small consequence, or decision. The harmed person must do more than endure.

Release should pay the correct debt: being believed, setting a boundary, regaining control, ending self-deception, leaving, ironic consequence, or proportionate exposure. Public humiliation is optional, not default.

## Genre Fit

- Drama: preserve relationship choice and consequence.
- Comedy: reduce harm and keep the frame recoverable.
- Horror: dangerous choices need a credible need and incomplete information.
- Romance: love does not erase agency or consent.
- Mystery: evidence handling must remain rational within bias.
- Foreknowledge/system: special rules remain explicit and limited.

## Breakers

- using `não tàn` to excuse arbitrary actions;
- everyone behaving absurdly;
- victim suffering only to extend runtime;
- social groups or vulnerability used as the cause of foolishness;
- each escalation supplied by coincidence;
- serious harm turned comic without distance;
- repeated kneeling/public revenge as payoff;
- intentionally sloppy narration or audio clarity;
- antagonist becomes incoherent only at the climax.
