# Deliberate Truyen-Rac Premise

This is a **modifier**, not an independent genre. It adjusts the dose of absurdity, the distance between listener and character, and the compression/release of frustration. It must not destroy base logic, flatten characters into lifeless tools, or force every story to end in public revenge.

Before applying it, read the shared coordination contract in [../audio-story-engagement/references/phoi-hop-skills.md](../audio-story-engagement/references/phoi-hop-skills.md). Default order:

1. `audio-story-engagement` controls narrative structure and workflow.
2. `audio-story-genre-*` defines the main genre reward.
3. This skill increases exaggeration and designs frustration, comedy, or catharsis.
4. `audio-story-premise-biet-truoc`, if present, defines special knowledge.
5. `audio-story-final-polish` edits last without erasing approved deliberate absurdity.

## 1. Activation Gate

Use this skill only when the user explicitly asks for signals such as:

- `truyện rác`, `truyện não tàn`, `tháo não`;
- impossibly foolish / infuriatingly foolish characters;
- rage bait, `truyện ức chế`;
- deliberately absurd or ludicrous situations;
- parody of an over-the-top drama trope.

Do not activate it merely because a draft has logic errors, unmotivated actions, or awkward style. Fix those with the base skill and `audio-story-final-polish`; do not legitimize bad writing as "truyen-rac."

Do not use derogatory cognition terms in narration as human diagnosis. If the user wants harsh colloquial flavor, place it in character speech only when context fits and safety risk is acceptable.

## 2. Choose The Mode

Identify the main experience before plotting. Modes may blend, but one must lead.

### 2.1 Frustration Waiting For Release

Listeners watch someone keep choosing wrong because of bias, with consequences falling on others. Main reward: **a boundary is set, truth is recognized, or proportionate consequence arrives**. Fits drama, romance, family, betrayal.

### 2.2 Absurdity For Laughter

Behavior is exaggerated, but harm level is reduced or comic distance is increased. Main reward: **ironic consequence, status reversal, or recognition of a pattern**. Fits situation comedy, satire, parody.

### 2.3 Looking Back At Blindness

The present narrator looks back at a past self with new understanding. Main reward: **recognition, responsibility, and change**, not merely mocking "past me was stupid." Fits romance, drama, first-person memory.

### 2.4 Exaggeration As Critique

Plot exaggerates a social vice, unwritten rule, or familiar trope. Main reward: **recognizing the absurd mechanism**, not venting at a vulnerable group. Fits satire and dark drama.

## 3. Premise Contract

A deliberate truyen-rac story must answer six design questions:

| Component | Design question |
|---|---|
| Skewed person | Who chooses wrongly or behaves excessively? |
| Root need | What are they protecting: face, status, love, belonging, money, belief? |
| Bias | How does that need distort facts? |
| Cost-bearer | Who is affected, and what concrete damage occurs? |
| Reality anchor | Which laws of the world still operate normally? |
| Reward | Are listeners waiting to laugh, see a boundary, see truth, or see consequence? |

Without **root need**, the character is a puppet. Without **reality anchor**, the whole world becomes arbitrary. Without **reward**, frustration piles up until listeners leave.

## 4. Design Foolishness With Cause

Use this chain:

> need -> bias -> wrong interpretation -> choice -> consequence -> self-justification or adjustment

Root needs with weight:

- Fear of being seen as an unfilial child, so they defend their mother against every sign.
- Fear of marriage failure, so each betrayal proof becomes "a misunderstanding."
- Need to prove they are smart, so admitting deception becomes too costly.
- Need money for a child's treatment, so they ignore signs that the house is wrong.

"He is just stupid" is not a motive.

The character can be competent at work but blind in a relationship with a parent. That contrast is usually more credible than incompetence everywhere. Each wrong choice should be a variation of the same distortion or a consequence of the previous wrong choice. If the character changes type of stupidity only to move plot, it is author error.

Evidence must fit genre scope:

- **Direct frustration:** listeners know more and watch the character bend facts.
- **Irony:** character has enough data but chooses ego-serving interpretation.
- **Mystery:** listener and character have partial pieces; frustration comes from handling, not hidden info.
- **Retrospective:** present narrator knows what past self missed.

In mystery, evidence must preserve fair play if promised. In horror, ambiguity may be part of fear.

Escalate by commitment, not sudden madness:

1. A wrong but understandable choice.
2. Character invests honor, money, or relationship into it.
3. New facts threaten self-image.
4. Admitting wrong becomes more expensive, so they justify harder.
5. Next choice creates undeniable consequence or cost.

## 5. Design Absurdity With Anchors

Absurdity may live in behavior, social convention, premise mechanics, or exaggeration level. Listeners still need to know **what is real in this world**.

Use anchors:

- Law, money, and evidence still create consequences.
- A family may cover something up, but outsiders do not automatically agree.
- A supernatural/system mechanism has consistent rules and limits.
- Characters may misread; narration does not change truth to save a twist.

Let the world react: awkward silence, rumors, lost trust, cost, procedure, avoidance, or resistance. A small concrete reaction is enough to mark the behavior as abnormal.

Keep one central image or behavior as the marketing/memory spine. Other absurd beats should deepen that spine, not compete for noise.

Vietnamese example:

> người mẹ giữ chiếc ghế trống cho cậu con trai đã bỏ nhà đi, rồi bắt con dâu mang thai đứng ăn cơm.

The empty chair becomes a symbol of favoritism, not just a shock beat.

## 6. Compress And Release Frustration

The release promise has many forms; it does not have to be "villain kneels and apologizes." It may be leaving, realizing, being believed, saving someone else, breaking a loop, or ending self-deception.

Track the frustration budget:

- **Intensity:** how painful is the harm?
- **Duration:** how long must listeners stay in injustice?
- **Agency:** can the harmed person observe, prepare, refuse, or act?

If all three are unfavorable for too long, change at least one: give preparation, one believer, a small consequence, a clue, or a meaningful decision.

Do not use a rigid "payoff every three minutes." Distance depends on story length, harm level, and genre reward.

Release the correct debt:

| Compressed debt | Fitting release |
|---|---|
| no one believes them | important person believes, or truth is credibly confirmed |
| boundary violated | character sets and keeps boundary |
| exploitation | benefit source is blocked; harmed person regains control |
| self-deception | character names truth and acts on it |
| comic absurdity | ironic consequence exposes contradiction |

Payoff may be public or private, legal/relational/moral/internal, sweet/bitter/funny. Main genre and approved ending decide the form.

## 7. Character And Agency

There is no mandatory role trio. These functions may be combined or absent:

- person choosing wrongly;
- person exploiting the blind spot;
- person bearing the cost;
- person who sees the issue;
- person/force making reality speak.

The harmed person must not only suffer. Agency may be recording evidence, stopping explanations to someone who refuses to listen, protecting a child, lying to buy time, asking for help, setting conditions, leaving, or choosing not to retaliate. The number of decisions depends on length; what matters is that decisions change causality or reveal character.

Wrong characters still need an inner life: ability, attachment, or fear outside the foolish beat. This does not excuse them; it gives consequence weight.

Do not equate vulnerability with foolishness. Avoid using mental illness, disability, age, gender, region, or class as a shortcut explanation for absurd behavior. Design specific need, power, and choice.

## 8. Combine With Genre And Premise

### Drama

Drama defines relationship debt and consequence. This modifier increases blindness/exaggeration. Do not force "everyone is equally wrong" when abuse or power imbalance is clear.

### Comedy

Reduce real harm, increase comic distance, and design immediate consequence. Use the comedy skill's benign-violation logic: behavior is off-norm but context remains safe enough to laugh.

### Horror

Wrong choices must arise from strong need, but the genre keeps ambiguity and threat. Example: staying in a suspicious cheap room because the deposit is the last money needed to keep child custody.

### Romance

Do not use "love makes them stupid" as the only motive. Show attachment need, relationship history, eroded boundaries, and the moment of regained choice. Respect consent and the right to leave.

### Mystery

Do not let characters destroy evidence or block investigation merely because the script needs it. Actions must pass through authority, interest, fear, or cognitive distortion. Do not use expressions/eye avoidance as lie detectors.

### Foreknowledge

`audio-story-premise-biet-truoc` defines what is known, from where, and how knowledge changes. There is no universal rule that foreknowledge must always be broken.

Combinations:

- Reincarnated narrator sees old family bias repeat and chooses a different response.
- Book-transmigrated character discovers the book called someone "stupid" because it omitted their motive.
- System rewards absurd choices, forcing a tradeoff between short-term benefit and real consequence.

## 9. Repair Examples

### From Random Stupidity To Causal Blindness

**Flat:**

> Camera quay rõ em gái lấy chiếc nhẫn. Chồng tôi vẫn bảo tôi vu oan cho cô ấy.

**Repaired:**

> Anh xem hết đoạn camera, nhưng dừng đúng ở khung hình con bé đặt tay lên hộp nhẫn.
>
> “Nó chỉ cầm lên xem.”
>
> Anh nói câu ấy quá nhanh. Ba năm trước, chính anh đã hứa trước mộ mẹ rằng sẽ không bao giờ bỏ mặc em mình thêm lần nữa. Nếu thừa nhận con bé lấy trộm, anh cũng phải thừa nhận lời hứa đó đã biến thành cái cớ.

Evidence is not denied by magic; it collides with a specific psychological debt.

### From Arbitrary World To Anchored Reaction

**Flat:**

> Mẹ chồng ném vali tôi ra đường. Cả nhà đều vỗ tay.

**Repaired:**

> Bà kéo chiếc vali qua bậc cửa. Không ai vỗ tay. Bố chồng cúi nhìn bát cơm, cô em chồng lùi điện thoại khỏi khung hình, còn chồng tôi đứng chắn lối nhưng không dám nhìn tôi. Sự im lặng của họ không bênh bà; nó chỉ cho thấy chẳng ai định ngăn bà.

Complicity remains infuriating, but each person reacts from position and fear.

### Payoff That Matches Debt

**Flat:**

> Tôi chiếu tất cả bằng chứng giữa tiệc cưới. Anh quỳ xuống xin tha trước hai trăm người.

**Repaired:**

> Tôi không mang đoạn ghi âm tới tiệc cưới. Tôi gửi nó cho luật sư, khóa tài khoản chung rồi để lại chìa khóa căn hộ trên bàn. Lần đầu tiên trong sáu năm, quyết định của tôi không cần anh tin mới có hiệu lực.

The debt is agency, so the payoff is regained agency; public humiliation is unnecessary.

## 10. Common Traps

| Trap | Repair |
|---|---|
| Using `não tàn` to hide logic errors | Rewrite need -> bias -> choice -> consequence |
| Everyone acts absurdly | Choose reality anchors and individual interests |
| Harmed person endures only to extend runtime | Give them observation, preparation, or choice |
| Humiliating antagonist every few minutes | Pay the right debt and vary reward by genre |
| Payoff always public kneeling apology | Choose payoff by harm, agency, and approved ending |
| Serious harm made comic | Adjust distance and safety; do not force laughter |
| Book-transmigration knows everything | Use the knowledge ledger from `audio-story-premise-biet-truoc` |
| Mystery treats eye avoidance as lying | Use testimony, interests, time, evidence, verifiable contradictions |
| Absurdity only gets louder | Each escalation increases cost, commitment, or exposes blind spot |
| Narration intentionally sloppy | Keep sentences clear, `xưng hô` correct, causality readable; the "trash" is in experience design, not broken craft |

## 11. Checklist

- [ ] Did the user explicitly request deliberate truyen-rac/rage-bait/absurdity?
- [ ] Is the main genre and genre reward identified?
- [ ] Mode chosen: frustration-release, absurd-laughter, retrospective blindness, or satire?
- [ ] Wrong behavior passes through need, bias, and concrete interpretation?
- [ ] The wrong character is consistent in blind spot but not flattened everywhere?
- [ ] Information level fits genre, especially mystery and horror?
- [ ] World has anchors and reactions with readable consequences?
- [ ] Harmed person retains meaningful agency?
- [ ] Frustration budget balances intensity, duration, and agency?
- [ ] Payoff pays the correct debt, not forced revenge/public shaming?
- [ ] If combined with foreknowledge, special knowledge has source, scope, and fallibility?
- [ ] No numeric quota replaces contextual judgment?
- [ ] Final polish preserves approved exaggerated flavor while fixing stiff prose, fake motive, and wrong address forms?
