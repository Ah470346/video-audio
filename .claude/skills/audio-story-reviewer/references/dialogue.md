# Dialogue and Vietnamese Address Forms

Use for dialogue value, voice, speaker clarity, and `xưng hô`.

## Keep or Compress

Keep direct speech when exact wording changes choice, knowledge, relationship, humor, intimacy, misunderstanding, or consequence. Summarize greetings, logistics, repeated arguments, known exposition, and filler.

Judge the exchange, not every line. Human speech may be partial, practical, evasive, late, mistaken, or unanswered.

## Natural Speech

Check:
- each line responds to what was actually said;
- vocabulary fits age, rank, education, region, intimacy, and pressure;
- characters do not all speak in narrator cadence;
- exposition is not recited between people who know it;
- professional/object metaphors are not forced;
- the next speaker answers the person, not merely completes a clever device;
- insight and apology are not implausibly complete.

## Single-Voice Speaker Clarity

Mentally remove quotation marks and paragraph breaks. Flag:
- a consequential line that could belong to either speaker;
- loss of ownership after a long turn, interruption, narrator insertion, or third-speaker entry;
- several same-gender characters carried by pronouns;
- dialogue that requires counting alternation;
- over-tagging or decorative gestures used as labels.

Repair with the smallest natural anchor: name, relation, title, purposeful action, light attribution, unique address form, narrator bridge, or speaker-specific knowledge.

## `Xưng Hô`

Track directionally: `A -> B` may differ from `B -> A`.

Check age, kinship, hierarchy, public/private context, intimacy, conflict, performance, and who else can hear. Address shifts need an emotional or strategic trigger. Asymmetry can be intentional; do not force reciprocity.

## Labels

- expository dialogue;
- exchange without value;
- interchangeable voice;
- false conflict;
- unclear speaker;
- speaker-chain ambiguity;
- address drift;
- over-tagging / under-tagging;
- decorative action beat;
- over-finished speech;
- aphorism tennis;
- authorial ventriloquism;
- instant psychological omniscience.

Recommend the smallest repair. Do not rewrite an entire scene unless requested.
