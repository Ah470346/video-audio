# Optional Literary Texture

Plain prose, rough prose, and no change are valid. This skill must never become a mandatory “make it literary” or “make it human” stage.

Run only after action, motive, orientation, and causality are clear. Prefer a named passage and named symptom from the developmental editor or user.

## First Rule — Remove Visible Technique

Before adding anything, test deletion or simplification. Common repairs include:
- remove explanation after an already effective line;
- remove a stock gesture, object, body symptom, balanced metaphor, or scene-closing thesis;
- replace generic abstraction with one exact fact already caused by the scene;
- reorder attention so it follows the narrator's current goal or avoidance;
- let a reaction arrive later through changed behavior;
- leave a truthful fragment or silence alone.

Do not add household objects, meals, rain, keys, phones, documents, chores, sensory lists, backstory, or small bodily actions as a default route to “life.” `audio-story-human-life` is retired and must not be recreated.

## Method

1. Lock POV, narrator distance, present pressure, genre function, and protected roughness.
2. Mark only supported genericity, over-explanation, or mechanical rhythm.
3. Choose the smallest repair: deletion, exact fact, reordered attention, direct emotion, omission, later consequence, rhythm change, or one owned image.
4. Run swap and portability tests: could another narrator or story use the passage unchanged?
5. Remove the change when it advertises technique, equalizes voices, completes a useful omission, or weakens audio clarity.
6. Increment revision/hash, invalidate stale receipts, and rerun full-draft development before clarity/final polish.

## May Change

- what this POV notices, misses, postpones, or refuses to formulate;
- order in which facts are admitted;
- a scene-specific practical or spatial fact that affects action;
- relationship-specific wording or `xưng hô`;
- direct emotion when symbolic choreography is weaker;
- one comparison with a recoverable literal target and clear ownership;
- a callback whose return changes choice or interpretation;
- sentence rhythm that follows attention and pressure.

## Reject

- quotas for images, motifs, callbacks, sensory details, fragments, or sentence variety;
- profession-derived metaphors by default;
- people treated as objects/systems without exact mapping;
- another speaker's perfect counter-metaphor;
- arbitrary realism props used to carry emotion;
- fragments or aphorisms added for prestige;
- ambiguity hiding actor, action, mechanism, or causality;
- any detail, gesture, or sentence skeleton borrowed from examples;
- smoothing every sentence to the same polished register.

The final story must still work through one voice. Do not delete audible anchors or complete semantic landings merely to appear subtle.
